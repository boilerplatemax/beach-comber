    Transform

    Scale

    FBX files record their unit. Mixamo and most Maya or 3ds Max files use centimeters, so they're scaled by 0.01.

    FBX files also record which way is up. Files saved Z-up (3ds Max and some other tools) would otherwise load lying down.

    Flip axis

    Flipping one axis mirrors the model, which also turns its faces inside out. Turn on Flip winding to fix that.

    Color

    Color source

    A textured part takes the texture's color at the middle of each triangle, in place of its material color.

    Default color

    Used where a part has no vertex colors and no material color.

    Color conversion

    Applies to colors read from the file, not the default color.
